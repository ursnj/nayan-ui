# Asset attribution

The game code, procedural car/tree models, race markers and particle/collision helpers are adapted from Playtura’s games library (`packages/games/src/games/car-racing` and `packages/games/src/games/shared`). No imported car GLB models are used.

Textures and WAV effects are copied from `packages/games/src/assets/textures` and `packages/games/src/assets/sfx` in the sibling Playtura checkout.

`fonts/Inter-SemiBold.ttf` is copied from Playtura’s games-library assets. Inter is by Rasmus Andersson and is distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Its license is included in `fonts/OFL.txt`.

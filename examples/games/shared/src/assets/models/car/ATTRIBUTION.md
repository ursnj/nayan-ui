# Car model

`car_body.glb`, `car_glass.glb` and `car_trim.glb` are Khronos' glTF sample [Car Concept](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/CarConcept), licensed [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) and built from a public-domain model by Unity Fan. The Nayan Game Engine example's `scripts/import-car.py` converted it: the interior and hidden parts are dropped, textures become flat colors, the paint (white, tinted per car), glass and trim are split into three models, and the mesh is simplified.

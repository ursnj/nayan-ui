# Asset attribution

`models/car_body.glb`, `models/car_glass.glb` and `models/car_trim.glb` are derived from Khronos Group's **Car Concept** sample:

- Source: https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/CarConcept
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- Original model credited by the Playtura importer to Unity Fan (public domain); the distributed Khronos sample is CC BY 4.0.
- Modifications in Playtura: removed interior/hidden parts, replaced textures/material extensions with flat colors, split body/glass/trim, rotated to face -Z, grounded at Y=0, and simplified with glTF-Transform/meshoptimizer. These modified files are reused here.

The tree, road/grass/facade/advert textures and WAV sound effects are copied from Playtura's engine example. They are generated by its `scripts/make-models.py`, `scripts/make-textures.py` and `scripts/make-sounds.py`.
